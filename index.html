The new org chart for agents?

Agents increasingly work vertically; integrating sophisticated tasks from idea to deployment, while our organisations were designed horizontally; task distribution, reviews, sign-offs, and steercos. That mismatch is the biggest blocker organisations face when trying to absorb the exponential capability of agents.

The orgs seeing success are using kanban and kaizen, building skills in delegation and discernment, running root-cause analysis, and growing organisational memory from conversations and actions.

Sound familiar?

It should, because the new org chart for agents dusts off the practices of lean, agile, and well-established leadership principles.
